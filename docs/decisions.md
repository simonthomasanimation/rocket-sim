- 2026-10-14 — Rails warp: ×100, ×1,000, ×10,000, ×100,000 above the physics ladder. A rails tick is one sixtieth of real time in which the craft is moved along its Kepler orbit (universal-variable propagation, exact) by rate/60 seconds in chunks of at most 60 s, handing over at a sphere-of-influence change and stopping at the edge of an atmosphere or 1 km above the ground; debris is left behind. Allowed only with the engine off, no parachute, clear of any atmosphere by 2 km and above a floor per body and level (Earth 14 / 50 / 200 / 500 km; Moon 5 / 20 / 100 / 500 km), and never within three ticks of a predicted hard event. The rails level rides in the recorded command (recording version 3), so a warped flight replays exactly; the replay shuttle itself stops at ×64.
